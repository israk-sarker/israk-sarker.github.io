Automated M365 Restoration & Verification Suite

A completed backup job alone doesn't prove that data can be restored when disaster strikes. Developed during my internship at Logos Technologies, this tool automatically tests restore points across Exchange Online (.msg), OneDrive, and SharePoint by performing local, non-destructive extraction from backup repositories, verifying cryptographic SHA-256 checksums, and generating client-ready audit reports.

  • Multi-Tenant Auto-Discovery: Discovers local VB365 organizations and jobs automatically without manual hardcoding.
  • Cryptographic Integrity Audit: Enforces non-zero byte checks, valid candidate sampling, and SHA-256 hashing on all extracted test items.
  • Client-Ready Audit Evidence: Produces structured JSON, HTML, plain text, and PDF reports with timestamps and sample details for compliance logs.
  • Portable Desktop GUI: Built with PySide6, featuring live process queue execution, organization workspace isolation, and automated retention cleanup.
